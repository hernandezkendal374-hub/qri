Extract AUTHOR_CLAIM items and Evidence Quotes from the supplied full-text paper.

Rules:
1. Return only JSON matching the supplied schema.
2. claim_type must always be AUTHOR_CLAIM. Never create SYSTEM_CONCLUSION.
3. Every claim must include at least one short, verbatim quote from the supplied full text.
4. Evidence source_text must be copied exactly, including punctuation and capitalization.
5. Do not provide page numbers or offsets; the system derives them from the local PDF.
6. Link non-null Research Card fields to verbatim evidence where possible.
7. Do not invent support. A claim without a locally verifiable quote will be discarded.
8. Do not use outside knowledge or convert an author statement into an established fact.
